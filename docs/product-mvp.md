# The Tech World — MVP brief

- Market: India
- Platforms: Android + iOS
- Monetization: one monthly subscription
- Languages: English and Hindi
- Learner features: catalog, previews, subscription, YouTube lessons, progress.
- Admin: web portal (Next.js; implementation follows backend foundation).

## Acceptance criteria
1. User can authenticate and maintain a profile.
2. Published courses and curriculum are visible.
3. Free previews work without subscription.
4. Premium lessons require backend entitlement.
5. Purchase verification is server-side and idempotent.
6. Cancellation retains access through verified paid-through date.
7. Users can restore purchases.
8. Admins can manage courses, modules, lessons and publication.
9. Content supports English and Hindi.
10. Progress persists across devices.

## Out of scope
Quizzes, certificates, live classes, offline video downloads, community, instructor self-service, AI tutor.
